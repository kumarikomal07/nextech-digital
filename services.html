FAQs

Questions About Working With Us

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Get in touch.

How do you price projects?

Most projects are priced as a fixed scope for a defined phase, or as a monthly team for ongoing product work. After an initial discovery call we provide a clear, written estimate.

How long does a typical project take?

A focused website or prototype can take a few weeks; larger platforms are delivered in phases over several months. We always aim to ship something usable early.

Can you work with our existing team and systems?

Yes. We regularly integrate with in-house teams, existing codebases and third-party systems, and can hand over fully documented work.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance, monitoring and continuous improvement plans tailored to the solution and your needs.

Who owns the code?

You do. On full payment, the intellectual property in the bespoke work we create for you is transferred to you, as set out in your contract.
