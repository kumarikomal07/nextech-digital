Illustrative project · concept demonstration, not a real client engagement

Business Portal Platform

A modern web platform to streamline business operations. Sector: Professional services.

Objectives

  • Bring clients, documents and requests into one secure portal
  • Reduce email back-and-forth for routine requests
  • Give managers a live view of work in progress

The challenge

Client information was spread across email, shared drives and spreadsheets, making it hard to see the status of any request.

Our solution

A role-based web portal with secure document exchange, request tracking, notifications and a management dashboard, integrated with existing accounting software.

Intended outcomes

  • Single source of truth for client requests
  • Self-service access for clients, around the clock
  • Clear reporting on workload and turnaround
Technologies used:Next.jsNode.jsPostgreSQLAWS

Key learning

Involving front-line staff in early prototypes surfaced edge cases that would otherwise have appeared after launch.

Healthcare Connect App

A patient-focused mobile app for appointments and care management. Sector: Healthcare.

Objectives

  • Let patients book and manage appointments on their phone
  • Send reminders to reduce missed appointments
  • Protect sensitive health information

The challenge

Appointment booking relied on phone calls during office hours, creating queues and missed appointments.

Our solution

  • Lower administrative load on reception teams

Cloud

Illustrative project · concept demonstration, not a real client engagement

Cloud Migration & Modernisation

A secure and scalable cloud infrastructure for business growth. Sector: Retail.

Objectives

  • Move on-premise workloads to the cloud with minimal downtime
  • Handle seasonal traffic peaks
  • Introduce automated deployments

The challenge

Ageing on-premise servers struggled during seasonal peaks, and every release required a manual, out-of-hours deployment.

Our solution

A phased migration to containerised services on AWS, infrastructure as code with Terraform, auto-scaling and a CI/CD pipeline with automated testing.

Intended outcomes

  • Infrastructure that scales automatically with demand
  • Repeatable, automated deployments
  • Improved monitoring and recovery
Technologies used:AWSDockerKubernetesTerraform

Key learning

Migrating a low-risk service first built team confidence and refined the runbook before critical systems moved.

AI & Automation

Illustrative project · concept demonstration, not a real client engagement

Intelligent Workflow Automation

An AI-powered solution to automate repetitive business processes. Sector: Financial services.

Objectives

  • Extract data from incoming documents automatically
  • Route work to the right team
  • Keep a human in the loop for exceptions

The challenge

Staff spent hours each day reading documents and re-keying information into back-office systems.

Our solution

A document-processing pipeline that classifies files, extracts key fields with AI, validates them against business rules and sends exceptions to a review queue.

Intended outcomes

  • Routine documents processed without re-keying
  • Exceptions flagged for human review
  • Full audit trail of automated decisions
Technologies used:PythonFastAPIPyTorchAzure

Key learning

Confidence thresholds and a clear review queue were key to staff trusting the automation.

Inventory & Operations Suite

Custom software connecting stock, orders and warehouse teams. Sector: Logistics.

Objectives

  • Real-time stock visibility across sites
  • Faster order picking
  • Replace paper pick lists

The challenge

Stock levels were updated manually at the end of each day, leading to overselling and slow fulfilment.

Our solution

A custom operations system with barcode scanning on handheld devices, live stock levels, order allocation rules and integrations with e-commerce channels.

Intended outcomes

  • Live stock levels across locations
  • Paperless picking with scanning
  • Fewer order errors
Technologies used:ReactJavaPostgreSQLDocker

Key learning

Designing for gloves and poor warehouse lighting changed the interface more than any desk-based review.
